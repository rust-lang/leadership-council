### "[launching-pad] Re-charter the Launching Pad team" leadership-council#170

- **Link:** https://github.com/rust-lang/leadership-council/issues/170
- **Labels:** A-teams, I-council-nominated

oli: This is Mara's. I'm not sure what to do here now, but I wanted to put it on the rotation because we dropped it.

TC: I'll give some background, as council membership has changed. Josh had raised an interesting point, regarding earlier council plans to do this recharter, asking where we had decided this. The answer, I think, is that we never did FCP it. We had taken it for granted after having discussed it. The original council charter had set out the launching pad as being temporary. The goal was to move everything out of it and then remove it. But what we had decided, in an earlier council, by wide consensus, was the idea that we would keep the launching pad around indefinitely and stop making it a goal to get rid of it. The idea was that there would continue to be some light outward pressure to move teams out of it that should be out of it, where they would make more sense elsewhere in the structure of the Project. But we had recognized that there was virtue in having a team to house cross-functional teams, as pseudo top-level teams that report directly to the council. We had suggested that we might rename it. But we were all happy keeping it around to serve that purpose. So that's some of the context in which this issue exists.

Rémy: Cool, thanks.

Josh: Part of the original intent was that even if we say there is an outward pressure to get teams out of the Launching Pad to get them to some other team, that we may need some larger organizational structure change. We have talked about having cross-functional / horizontal teams and having some structure for that. I'm not arguing against the idea of having a place for new teams, but the overall organizational and structural change we may need to make would make it less likely to happen if we keep the team and do this on a team-by-team basis.

Manish: I'd like as much of the background that was talked here on the issue. The Rust Project has been bad at horizontals always. These have always caused problems in governance. This is something that the core team struggled with. There were never really any major attempts to solve that. The Launching Pad was kind of the first attempt in that direction. I'll write a comment on the issue.

Rémy: TC, do you have links to the meeting notes where this background discussion happened?

TC: It's been on my mind to look for those. Some of this happened before our minutes became better (because they were before we had the PMs). So some of these minutes are likely to be a bit rough. But it's worth looking at them. People such as Mark and Eric Holk are others who were there, who would remember this clearly, and that we could talk with to reconstruct this history if needed.

Rémy: Cool. It's good we'll have some background so we can discuss this later, with Mara.
