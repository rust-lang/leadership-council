### New group: `security`, with subgroups: `security-response`, `project-security`, `ecosystem-security` [leadership-council#352](https://github.com/rust-lang/leadership-council/issues/352)

Pete: Thanks to Jess for putting the issue together. Jess, would you like to give us an intro on this?

Jess: Historically, we've had `security-response`, focused on incident response on the Rust toolchain, and then we've also had security engineers in the Rust Foundation building systems; these are not the most visible to the Project. This is an attempt to create better entry points for people interested in this work to contribute without needing to be inside the circle of trust. This is also an opportunity for the Foundation to be more integrated with the Project, and have people to help them with that work, while also bringing in new scope such as hardening GitHub, etc. There's work that the `crates.io` team and the infra team have held, by default, that perhaps are not people's favorite things to hold. So it's subtle, and there's a lot of overlap with existing groups. The charter attempts to talk about the decision-making structure. The TL;DR is that this group offers advice but doesn't own other people's stuff. My hope is that it will harness energy, be a way to attract funding, and make security work more accessible. People have signed on, and there's interest. I have funding to work on this, so I'm not too worried about managing this or stretching myself too thin.

Manish: For a long time, we've been doing reactive security. Which is good, and we should be doing that. And the people doing reactive security shouldn't be on the hook for doing proactive security too. I like the way Jess has split this up, and I want us to have a better place for security stuff to be discussed in the Project.

Pete: I'd suggest that we read through this async and then discuss it next time.

Mark: Feel free to leave your thoughts on the issue, but I think we've been suggesting that charters of this scope go to an RFC. This feels significant as a restructuring. I was broadly happy with an earlier draft, and I think we should be able to FCP it fairly quickly.

Jess: Happy to open an RFC. To clarify, this does not propose a top-level team, but one under `launching-pad`. I'd like to see how this operates first and reevaluate later, e.g., after 6 months.

Pete: Could you drop this as its own topic in the `#council` channel?

Jess: Can do.
